Guofan YU is currently a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at Hong Kong Baptist University, supervised by Prof. Amelie Chi Zhou.

He received his B.Eng. degree in Computer Science from Beihang University (北航计算机学院). His research interests include ML systems, high-performance computing, and distributed systems, with a focus on communication-efficient full-graph GNN training and SmartNIC / DPU offloading.
